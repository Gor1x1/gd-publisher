GD Publisher

GD Publisher is a personal publishing tool of Georgi Darbinyan, a content creator from Armenia. It publishes Georgi's own posts and videos to his own social media accounts through the official APIs of the platforms: Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

  • Georgi prepares a post (an image or a video and its caption).
  • The tool sends him a preview in a private Telegram bot.
  • Nothing is published until Georgi personally approves that specific post for that specific platform.
  • After publishing, the tool reads the post back through the platform API to confirm it was published correctly.

The tool has no other users. It does not publish to anyone else's accounts, does not post automatically and does not collect data about other people.
