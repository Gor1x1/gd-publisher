Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

GD Publisher ("the tool") is a personal publishing tool operated by Georgi Darbinyan ("the owner"). Only the owner uses it, and only with his own accounts. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the tool accesses

When the owner connects one of his accounts, the platform gives the tool an access token with the permissions the owner approved. With it the tool uses only:

  • Basic account information of the owner's account (account ID, name or username, profile picture), to confirm that the correct account is connected.
  • Publishing permissions, to upload the owner's own images and videos and publish them with a caption.
  • Read-back of the published post (ID, link, caption, status), to confirm that the publication was successful.

Platforms: Facebook Pages, Instagram and Threads (Meta), YouTube (Google; the tool uses YouTube API Services) and TikTok.

2. Data the tool does not collect

The tool does not collect, store or process data about other people: no followers, no comments, no messages, no contacts and no analytics about third parties. It does not use cookies, advertising or tracking.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and publication records are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in files outside of any public repository. They are never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. The owner can disconnect an account at any time by deleting its stored token. Access can also be revoked directly on the platform:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Threads: Threads Settings → Account → Website permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

5. Third-party policies

Use of the platforms is also governed by their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service, Meta Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page with its effective date.
